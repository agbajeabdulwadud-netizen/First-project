What to Bring to Your Outpatient Visit

  • Valid photo identification (National ID, Driver's License, or International Passport).
  • Previous diagnostic lab reports, CT/X-Ray imaging films, and medical summaries.
  • Current medication list with exact dosage schedules in original containers.
  • Insurance card, HMO membership verification, or corporate guarantee letters.

Visiting Hours & Family Policies

General Inpatient Wards: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily.

Intensive Care Unit (ICU): Restricted to immediate family (strictly 1 visitor at a time under physician supervision).

Billing Transparency & Insurance Support

Doctor Cares supports transparent fee schedules before elective procedures. For questions regarding HMO verification, contact our billing desk at +234 904 240 7509.
