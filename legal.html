Institutional Transparency

Governance, Privacy & Accessibility

1. Data Privacy Notice & Patient Confidentiality

Doctor Cares Hospital respects patient privacy and aligns with international data protection standards. All submissions on this demonstration platform are handled in simulated local state mode and are not stored permanently or shared with third parties.

2. Terms of Clinical Web Use

The information provided on this platform is for informational and navigational purposes. It does not constitute medical diagnosis, surgical consultation, or prescription advice. Clinical care requires formal evaluation by our medical faculty.

3. Accessibility Statement (WCAG 2.2 Level AA)

Doctor Cares Hospital is committed to digital inclusivity. Our platform incorporates high-contrast color palettes (minimum 4.5:1, with 10.4:1 body text), complete keyboard focus trapping on modals, skip links, and semantic heading outlines.

To report accessibility barriers, contact our technical coordinator at info@doctorcares.example.
